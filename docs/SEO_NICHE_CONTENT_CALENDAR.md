# SEO Niche Content Calendar

**Product:** HomeCheff Studio  
**Date:** June 2026  
**Horizon:** 12 months · 4 waves  
**Inventory:** 58 landings · 112 help · 42 tutorials

---

## Publishing principles

1. **P0 outcome landings first** — emotional + commercial niches  
2. **Help within 2 weeks** of each landing — capture long-tail  
3. **Tutorials after help cluster** — HowTo schema  
4. **Niche hub goes live** when ≥3 guides in pillar are ready  
5. **No batch of 10 thin pages** — max 4 landings/week with full quality gate
