# Help Center Content Roadmap

**Goal:** 50–100 quality articles over 12–18 months  
**Current:** 10 live articles  
**Base URL:** `/help`

---

## Content principles

1. **Answer real user tasks** — not keyword stuffing  
2. **Link to product surfaces** — Editor, Studio, Motion, Pricing  
3. **Cross-link related articles** — same category + workflow chains  
4. **Include screenshots** when UI steps matter (future enhancement)  
5. **Update when pricing/features change** — credit costs must match catalog
