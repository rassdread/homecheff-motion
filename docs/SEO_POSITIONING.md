# HomeCheff Studio — Competitive SEO Positioning

How HomeCheff Studio differs from major alternatives. Use this narrative in future comparison landers and help content — **not** as thin doorway pages at launch.

---

## Positioning statement

**HomeCheff Studio is an AI production line** — not a single-purpose generator. Creators build assets in **Editor**, plan stories in **Studio**, animate in **Motion**, and ship versions through **Publish**, with transparent **Studio Credits** and a shared **Library**.

---

## vs. Canva

| Dimension | Canva | HomeCheff Studio |
|-----------|-------|------------------|
| Core strength | Design templates, social graphics | End-to-end video production workflow |
| AI video | Add-on feature | Native Motion pipeline (image-to-video) |
| Story continuity | Limited character consistency | Studio character identity + storyboards |
| Output | Static + simple video | Versioned video with subtitles, voice, translation |
| Pricing model | Per-seat / Pro tiers | Credits + subscriptions with per-action transparency |

**Unique angle:** "Design in Canva, produce in HomeCheff" — HomeCheff owns the moving, voiced, localized output.
