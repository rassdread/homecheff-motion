HomeCheff notes:

- Characters: model and UI exist. Passion masters exist. Voice profiles on those three are empty.
- Video: Vidu path exists. Kling and Runway flags are not live adapters.
- Timeline: ordered scenes and a rendered MP4. No shared multitrack editor. Cell is NOT_SUPPORTED for a timeline, not for export.

- Pricing: HC rules and some confirms exist. Not every action shows a quote first.

## Differentiation

HomeCheff can own the film, the characters, the Dutch subtitle, the HC ledger, and the marketplace brand in one account. None of the generators above are the HomeCheff wallet. The gap is the timeline and an assistant that spends only after approval. Copy InVideo’s single project and Descript’s shared timeline. Do not copy a new brand or a second media library.
