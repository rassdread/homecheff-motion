# Competitor research

Access date: **2026-10-19**. Only public pages fetched that day are treated as verified. Memory is not a source. Conflicts are left as conflicts.

## Runway

- Purpose: generate and extend short shots. Target: creators who start from an image or a prompt.
- Workflow: Gen-4.5 text-to-video and image-to-video, 2–10 seconds, 720p, 24/25 fps. The image is the first frame. Longer films are assembled from character and environment plates, not one native long timeline in the docs that were read.
- Character consistency: Gen-4 References, up to 3 images.
- Pricing detail verified: 12 credits per second on the help article that was read.
- Sources: https://help.runwayml.com/hc/en-us/articles/46974685288467 (Gen-4.5), https://help.runwayml.com/hc/en-us/articles/48324313115155, https://help.runwayml.com/hc/en-us/articles/26871350018835, https://help.runwayml.com/hc/en-us/articles/40042718905875
- Not verified: a full multitrack editor, Dutch subtitles, or an agent that spends credits across a project.

## Kling AI

- Purpose: shot generation and video editing inside Kling.
- Official user guide for Kling 4.0 dated 2026-10-14: text, image, first-and-last frame, up to 10 keyframes, omni reference up to 15 inputs, 3–30 seconds, video extension toward 2 minutes, stereo.
- Membership page: yearly Standard $79.20 / 660 credits, Pro $293.04 / 3000, Premier $728.64 / 8000.
- Conflict: the feature page calls some 4K/HDR “coming soon”. The API pricing page lists older 2.5–3.0 models, not a complete 4.0 table. A point-policy snippet of $1 = 66 credits conflicts with membership copy of $1 per 100 credits. Pricing detail is **partial**.
- Sources: https://kling.ai , https://klingai.com (membership), Kling 4.0 user guide linked from the product on 2026-10-19.
- Not verified: project-owned character bible across films, or an approval ledger comparable to HC.

## Luma

- Docs: Ray2 and ray-flash-2, text and image to video, keyframes, resolutions from 540p to 1080p and a 4k parameter, 5 second example.
- FAQ and changelog (2025-01-15): 5–10 seconds, extend to 30 seconds, keyframes, loop, camera motion, audio via prompt, paid subscribers.
- Conflict: https://lumalabs.ai/ray2 still described image-to-video as “coming soon” while docs and FAQ describe it as available. Both statements are recorded. Capability is **partial** until that page is reconciled.
- Sources: https://docs.lumalabs.ai , https://lumalabs.ai/ray2
- Not verified: a film timeline or character library.

## Pika

- Video Studio: up to 50 referenced assets and 30-second multi-shot with sound, on the product pages read.
- Pika 2.5: prompt 5 seconds, image 5 or 10 seconds, 2–5 keyframes, 720p/1080p, silent renders, soundtrack separate. Character Studio exists.
- Pricing page: Free, Starter $10, Creator $35, Fancy $95–$880.
- Conflict: FAQ still describes older 2.2 credit tables and a free 150 credits. That FAQ is **unknown** relative to the current price page.
- Sources: https://pika.art , https://create.pika.art
- Not verified: owner-scoped project accounting.

## InVideo

- Purpose: prompt to a finished editable video (script, stock, voice, subtitles, music), then a timeline and text-command edits.
- Help article: manual timeline editing does not consume credits.
- Pricing page read: Starter about $10 per seat per month billed annually, 400 credits; higher tiers with 2000 credits and above.
- Model names on marketing pages (Seedance, Kling, Veo, ElevenLabs) are InVideo’s vendor claims, not re-tested.
- Sources: https://invideo.io , https://help.invideo.io
- Useful lesson: one project contains script, media, and timeline. Generation and editing are the same file.

## Descript

- Purpose: edit spoken video by editing the transcript.
- Timeline has blade, slip, and range tools. Transcription is not metered on the plans described; music lyrics are unsupported; files over 15 hours fail.
- Underlord (beta) can captions, clips, translate, sound, slides-to-video, and web search. The help text says it can be wrong. It is a co-editor on existing media, not a film generator.
- Sources: https://help.descript.com
- Useful lesson: AI edits the same timeline the user can edit by hand.

## CapCut

- Multi-track timeline (video, audio, image, text), trim and split, auto captions, music and SFX, templates, long-to-short, image-to-video and text-to-video tools, avatars.
- Some feature pages are thin. Specific model names such as Dreamina Seedance are marketing claims and are **not verified** as limits.
- Source: https://www.capcut.com
- Useful lesson: simple default tools, advanced tools behind the same timeline.

## Adobe Firefly

- Help pages updated 2026-06-16, 2026-08-07, and 2026-09-18: text and keyframe image to video, 24 fps, camera motion, partner models (Kling 2.5/3.0, Ray2/Ray3, Runway Gen-4.5, Veo, Gemini).
- Firefly video editor beta: timeline, voiceover record, auto reframe, generate speech and music.
- AI Assistant beta plans and executes multi-step workflows. Availability varies by region. Commercial-safety wording is Adobe’s claim and was not independently tested.
- Source: https://helpx.adobe.com
- Useful lesson: the assistant executes inside the product, and partner models are named before they run.

## ElevenLabs

- Docs: TTS, music, sound effects, dubbing. One page says 3,000+ community voices. ElevenCreative copy says 10,000+ premade voices. Those numbers are **not merged**.
- Dubbing covers many languages and tries to keep the speaker. Output is often a preview, not a production master.
- Studio’s own catalog size (about 20,272 eligible voices) is a HomeCheff database count, not ElevenLabs’ public library size.
- Source: https://elevenlabs.io/docs
- Role for HomeCheff: voice, music, and SFX provider. Not the film editor.

## What to learn

InVideo shows idea → assembled video in one project. Descript shows manual and AI edits on the same timeline. Firefly shows an assistant that must name the model and the cost before it runs. Runway, Kling, Luma, and Pika show reference images for character consistency. HomeCheff already has the reference images. It does not yet have the shared timeline or the executing assistant.
