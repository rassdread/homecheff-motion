# SEO Niche Expansion Roadmap

**Product:** HomeCheff Studio  
**Date:** June 2026  
**Sprint:** Niche audience expansion — outcome-driven SEO

---

## Executive summary

Generic AI video tools compete on **“AI video generator.”** HomeCheff wins on **outcome intent**: parents, authors, teachers, D&D groups, musicians, local businesses, hobbyists, families preserving memories, aspiring animators, and community organizers.

**Core positioning (every niche page):**

```
Idea → World → Characters → Voices → Scenes → Video → Translation → Publishing
```

Everything from one studio — story-first, not clip-first.

**Sibling docs:** `SEO_NICHE_KEYWORD_MAP.md` · `SEO_NICHE_INTERNAL_LINKING.md` · `SEO_NICHE_CONTENT_CALENDAR.md`

